GOOD FAITH PARADIGM • INVESTOR DEMONSTRATION

An interactive executive demonstration featuring the vision of Good Faith Paradigm (@GparadigmOrgLove): Sacred Sound synthesis, autonomous Urban Air Mobility (eVTOL), and Last-Mile Electric Micro-Transit connecting to world-class entertainment venues.

WebXR Mode (Quest / PC VR)

  • Enter VR: Click the ENTER VR button below.
  • Walk & Turn: Left stick walks; right stick snap-turns.
  • Boarding: Aim laser & click trigger or press A near eVTOL or Rover.
  • Vehicle Flight / Drive: Left stick accelerates/climbs; right stick steers.
  • Track Control: A / X play-pause, B / Y next, stick click prev.

Desktop First-Person Controls

  • Look & Walk: Click screen to lock mouse. W A S D to walk / drive.
  • Board & Exit: Press E near eVTOL or Ground Rover to board/exit.
  • eVTOL Flight: Hold Space for vertical lift, W cruise thrust.
  • Music & Media: Press Space to Play/Pause on foot, N Next, P Prev.
  • Teleport Hubs: Use bottom-right buttons to jump across campus anytime.
